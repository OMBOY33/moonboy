How long does it take to go live?
Twelve weeks, median. A voice AI deployment like Olivia can go live in six. We don't do year-long projects — we ship the top thing first, measure it, and compound from there. Every week has a named deliverable.
Do we need to provide data scientists or ML engineers?
No. We embed a senior pod with your team. You provide domain expertise and a product owner. We transfer knowledge as we go — at handover, your team owns the system, not us.
How does Olivia's voice work?
Low-latency STT/TTS pipeline with interruption handling, turn-taking, and intent classification. Production deployments run on Twilio with sub-300ms round-trips. The demo on this page uses your browser's en-GB speech engine. Voice, persona, and vocabulary can be customised to match your brand.
Can you work with regulated industries?
Yes — four current clients are FCA-regulated. We build compliance-grade from day one: full audit trails, eval harnesses, human-in-the-loop gates, data residency controls. Financial services, healthcare, and legal are our most active verticals.
What happens at handover?
Full runbooks, on-call documentation, trained internal team, 12 months of support. 100% of our engagements have been fully transferred. We are paid to leave — and we always do. You own the system, not a Colab notebook.
